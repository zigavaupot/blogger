In this series, I am looking at how AI capabilities are becoming part of the Oracle Analytics experience: from asking questions in natural language, to using AI directly inside workbooks, and then extending the experience with more focused AI Agents.

Oracle Analytics Meets AI: Talk to Your Data with Ask BI

Ask BI brings a conversational experience to the Oracle Analytics search bar. It allows users to ask natural-language questions, refine the analysis step by step, and turn answers into workbook-ready insights.

Oracle Analytics Meets AI: From Workbook Assistant to AI Agents

This post continues the story inside Oracle Analytics workbooks, where the AI Assistant helps users create and refine visualizations. It also introduces AI Agents as standalone, context-aware assistants that can combine data, instructions, and knowledge documents.
